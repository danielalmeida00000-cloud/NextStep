Car Poster Pro

Post dealership inventory to Facebook Marketplace in minutes, not hours. Built for individual salespeople.

See How It Works

How it works

1. Load Inventory

Upload a simple CSV from your dealership or use the sample. Select the cars you want to post.

2. Generate Captions

Choose a style (Professional, Casual, or Short) and instantly get ready-to-copy titles and descriptions with photos.

3. Fill & Post

Use the Chrome extension to help fill the Marketplace form while you stay logged in. You review and click Publish.

Why salespeople like it

  • Cuts listing time from 10–15 minutes per car to under a minute
  • Lower cost than most existing tools ($29–$49/month target)
  • You stay in control — no fully automatic posting
  • Works with the inventory you already have

This is a working prototype. Download the package, run the local app, and test it with your own inventory.
