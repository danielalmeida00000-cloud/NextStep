NextStep Automotive Solutions extension

1. Open chrome://extensions
2. Turn on Developer mode
3. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder
4. Open the NextStep Automotive Solutions desk once, so the extension knows which tab is yours
5. On a dealer inventory page, click the extension and choose Pull this page
   (a “Send to NextStep Automotive Solutions” button also shows up on vehicle pages)
6. On the desk, open the car and click Fill on Facebook
7. Check the Marketplace form. You press Publish. The extension never does.
